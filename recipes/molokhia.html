North Africa, Egypt

Molokhia

A comforting Egyptian soup of finely chopped jute leaves simmered in chicken broth. Its signature finish is the ta'leya: garlic and ground coriander fried in ghee and stirred in at the last moment. Served with rice or bread and the chicken from the broth.

Time 45 min

Serves 4

Ingredients

4
  • Minced molokhia (frozen or fresh) 400 g
  • Chicken broth 4 cups
  • Garlic, crushed 8 cloves
  • Ground coriander 2 tsp
  • Ghee or butter 2 tbsp
  • Cooked chicken to serve
  • Salt, lemon, rice or bread to taste

Method

1. Heat the broth

Bring the chicken broth to a gentle simmer and season with salt.

2. Add the molokhia

Stir in the molokhia and bring it back to a gentle simmer for about 5 minutes. Don't let it boil hard or it will separate.

3. Make the ta'leya

Fry the garlic in ghee until golden, then add the coriander for 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. Serve

Stir the hot ta'leya into the soup. Listen for the sizzle! Serve with rice or bread and the chicken.
